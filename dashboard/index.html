How the value is built

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × age × breadth × quality

  • Category multiple — the median mcap / TVL of the tokened protocols in the same category, computed at run time from this snapshot (global median when a category has fewer than 5 tokened comparables), clamped to 0.02–1.00. MCap here is circulating market cap, not FDV, so the value is a circulating-mcap equivalent; a new token’s FDV would typically run 2–3× higher.
  • Growth — 1 + 0.5 × clamp(7-day TVL change / 25%, −1, +1); a protocol growing 25%+ in a week earns the full 1.5×, one shrinking fast falls to 0.5×.
  • Age — from listed_at to the snapshot date: under 6 months 0.85×, 6–12 months 0.95×, 1–4 years rising to 1.10×, then fading back toward 0.85× after 4 years without a token.
  • Breadth — 1 + 0.08 × ln(chains), capped at 1.25×.
  • Quality — 0.5× for Risk Curators and Onchain Capital Allocators: a curator’s vaults belong to the underlying strategies, so a curator token would not capture their TVL. Everything else 1.0×.

Who counts as a launch candidate

A protocol records no token (gecko_id and symbol both null) and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades: category not Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Cross Chain Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC or CEX; name with no whole word wrapped / staked / staking, no trailing version marker, and no exchange prefix. The 40-protocol watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling — this board ranks every candidate the snapshot holds, 100+ rows.

What this board does not know

The snapshot carries no fees, revenue, users, funding, audits or points programs, so none of those enter the model. Gas figures in the farm plans are the builder’s November 2026 estimates, not snapshot data. Farm plans position a reader for a possible launch; they never promise an airdrop, a token amount, or a date.
